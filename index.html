Referral Tools

Built for a Case Manager Conversation

Use this section during a lunch-and-learn, adjuster meeting, or field visit to quickly explain conditions treated, treatment options, and what information helps the referral move smoothly.

▤

Referral Essentials

Helpful referral details include the claim number, employer, adjuster, authorization status, injury date, body part, prior treatment notes, and current work-status needs.

◉

Imaging & Records

MRI, CT, X-ray reports, operative notes, injection history, therapy notes, and prior specialist records can help the provider quickly determine the next clinical step.

☑

Conditions Treated

Total Spine & Orthopedics treats neck pain, back pain, bulging and herniated discs, sciatica, spinal stenosis, pinched nerves, SI joint pain, whiplash, joint pain, knee pain, hip pain, shoulder pain, foot and ankle conditions, and more.

⚕

Treatment Options

Care may include non-surgical treatment, physical therapy, diagnostic imaging, epidural steroid injections, facet injections, medial branch blocks, radiofrequency ablation, nerve block injections, discectomy, endoscopic spine surgery, minimally invasive surgery, SI joint injections, and orthopedic procedures.
