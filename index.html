Pillar 01 — Return to Work

Smaller incisions. Shorter recoveries. Less lost time.

Our ultra‑minimally invasive approach means many injured workers walk in and walk out the same day — and get back to modified or full duty sooner than traditional open surgery allows.

  • Tiny incisions reduce tissue damage, blood loss, and infection risk.
  • Most patients go home the same day and begin recovery immediately.
  • Less downtime translates directly into fewer indemnity weeks.
  • We start with non‑surgical options: physical therapy, injections, and regenerative care.
  • Cost‑effective, appropriate treatment that documents a clear progression of care.
  • Surgical intervention is reserved for cases that genuinely require it.
  • Prompt evaluations keep claims moving and prevent avoidable delays.
  • Timely imaging and diagnostics establish an accurate diagnosis early.
  • Two Central Florida locations shorten travel and wait times.
